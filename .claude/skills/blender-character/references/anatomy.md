# Anatomy cheat sheet (for stylised realism)

## Head (Loomis / Asaro planes)
- Eyes on the half-height line of the head (chin to skull top). Head width ≈ 5 eye widths,
  with one eye width between the eyes.
- Face thirds: hairline → brow, brow → nose base, nose base → chin.
- Mouth line about a third of the way from the nose base to the chin. Mouth corners line up
  with the pupils (relaxed) or the inner irises.
- Ears between the brow line and the nose base, starting just behind the jaw hinge, tilted
  back about 15°.
- Nose width ≈ the distance between the eyes. Wings sit on the cheek plane, the tip projects
  2.5 to 3 cm (caricature up to 4).
- Planes: forehead front + temples, brow ridge, eye sockets (a depression, not a hole),
  cheekbones, the muzzle barrel (mouth), chin box, jaw angle. Stylised realism reads when the
  planes turn sharply enough to catch light.

## Eyes
- Eyeball ≈ 24 to 25 mm. The upper lid covers the top of the iris (a fully visible iris reads
  as surprise), the lower lid touches its bottom.
- Lids have thickness (2 to 3 mm) and wrap the ball. The upper lid crease sits 8 to 10 mm
  above the lash line. Old age brings hooded lids, bags and drooping outer corners.
- Gaze: both eyes converge on one point. Divergent eyes read as broken.

## Ageing (what to push for old characters)
Bigger nose and ears, hollow temples and cheeks, prominent brow ridge, hooded lids, under-eye
bags, deeper nasolabial folds, jowls, thinner lips, sparse or white hair, bushy brows,
hunched upper back with forward head, sloped shoulders, a slight belly with thin limbs,
knobbly finger joints and visible veins and tendons.

## Body proportions (in heads, adult)
| | Realistic | Heroic | Stylised hero |
|---|---|---|---|
| height | 7.5 | 8 | 6 to 6.5 |
| shoulder width | 2 (m) / 1.7 (f) | 2.3 | 2 to 2.6 (pushed) |
| crotch | 4 from top | 4 | 3.3 to 3.5 |
| fingertips (arms down) | mid-thigh | mid-thigh | mid-thigh |
| elbow | waist | waist | waist |
| knee | 6 from top | 6 | ~5 |
Joints sit inside the limb, not on the surface: knee pivot ≈ 1/3 from the front, elbow at the
back, shoulder inside the deltoid ball.

## Hands
- Hand length ≈ face length (chin to hairline). The palm ≈ the middle finger length.
- Finger segments shrink by ~0.7 each. Knuckles form an arc and the middle finger is longest.
- The thumb starts at the heel of the palm, not at the side of the knuckles.
- Relaxed hands curl more towards the pinky. Stylised heroes get hands 1.2 to 1.4×.

## Posture tells personality
Confident: chest up, chin level, weight on one leg. Old: hunched, forward head, knees soft,
small steps. Shy: shoulders in, head down, arms crossing the body. Aggressive: forward lean,
low centre, wide stance. Smug: back lean, chin up, arms folded or hands behind the back.
